# Outreach Angles

## Risk Angle
Most lending decisions rely on reported numbers that aren’t re-verified.
Even one miscalculation (like DSCR) can pass through unnoticed.

## Efficiency Angle
Instead of manually double-checking numbers,
Lumen runs a second-pass verification instantly.

## Compliance Angle
There’s no clear audit trail for how AI-assisted decisions are validated.
Lumen logs every output with evidence and timestamps.

## Reality Trigger
Employees are already using AI tools informally.
Unverified outputs are entering decision workflows today.

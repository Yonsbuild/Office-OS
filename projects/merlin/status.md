# Merlin Status

- Processes large volumes of legal documents (PDF, DOCX, audio, email)
- Extracts entities, events, and timelines
- Performs multi-pass analysis to detect contradictions
- Runs verification checks on claims and references
- Produces structured, defensible reports with source grounding
- Maintains audit trail with evidence and traceability

Current limitations:
- Complex system (not lightweight like Lumen)
- Requires document ingestion (not instant input)
- Not packaged as a simple plug-and-play tool yet

# Outreach Angles

## Risk Angle
Legal teams rely on manual review of large document sets.
Critical contradictions and weak points can be missed.

## Scale Angle
As case volume increases, it becomes harder to maintain quality across all materials.
Merlin processes and analyzes large datasets consistently.

## Verification Angle
Most AI tools generate outputs but don’t verify them.
Merlin adds a second-pass verification layer.

## Reality Trigger
As AI is introduced into legal workflows, unverified outputs create risk.
Verification will become necessary, not optional.
